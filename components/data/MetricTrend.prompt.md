A compact metric card with mini bars (one highlighted) or a line, plus a trend pill. It fits two across at 375px.
```jsx
<MetricTrend label="Paid so far" value={133200} currency="AED" data={[14800,14800,14800]} delta="14,800 this month" trend="up" sentiment="good" />
```
`trend` sets the arrow and `sentiment` sets the color.
